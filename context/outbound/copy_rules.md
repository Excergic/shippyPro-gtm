# Independent audit copy contract

These are proposed messages for a ShippyPro-operated campaign, written as an independent ghost audit. The author does not represent ShippyPro. No sender identity, mailbox, approval to send, sent activity or response is implied. A real campaign owner would need to adapt and approve the copy before use.

Each contact receives four proposed emails at business-day offsets 0, 4, 9 and 16 after hypothetical activation. There is no activation date. Touch 1 connects a documented business change to the recipient's role. Touch 2 introduces a specific diagnostic question. Touch 3 offers a relevant ShippyPro product scenario and the verified public demo form. Touch 4 closes the loop without manufactured urgency. Stop after a reply, unsubscribe, bounce or meeting in a real campaign; audit review does not authorize sending.

Keep messages short, useful and transparent about ShippyPro. Ask about potential operational issues rather than asserting the recipient has them. Never pretend to have inspected internal systems, seen private metrics, met the recipient or completed an assessment for them. Do not promise a particular saving, integration, SLA, geographic carrier coverage or timeline. Do not imply ShippyPro replaces an OMS, WMS, freight platform, cold-chain system or 3PL.

Personalization uses company events, current professional role, and attributable business statements. Do not use unrelated personal interests, family, sensitive attributes, copied praise or inferred psychology. A liked/reposted item is not necessarily authored by the recipient. Old posts cannot be described as recent. Partial public research remains an explicit gap. Conflicting roles or regional scope must stay visible as review flags.

Draft bodies contain no fake signatures or invented company affiliation. The demo CTA points to ShippyPro's public request form, not a live calendar integration. Each touch carries source references outside the message body for the auditor. An operational owner must add a truthful sender identity and appropriate contact preferences before real use.
